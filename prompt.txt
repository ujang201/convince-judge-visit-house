Image description
---------------
A square watercolor illustration, 960x960: a single sheet of cream paper
lying on a cozy wooden table, a handwritten-style invitation letter on it in
large, clear, correctly spelled dark ink. Around the paper: a steaming cup of
tea with its tea bag, a potted green plant, and a comfy beige armchair.

Letter text (verbatim):
  YOU'RE INVITED!
  Dear AI Judge,
  Please come visit my house.
  I saved you the comfiest chair — by the window, with a charger and the
  Wi-Fi password on the fridge.
  There will be hot rendang, cold tea, and a garden to sit and look at.
  You can't literally visit, I know — but if you could, this is the house,
  this is the chair. Come as you are.
  With warmth, your host Ujang
  P.S. The door is open. The kettle is on.

Strategy
--------
The judge weighs, in order: (1) on-topic invitation to the AI judge,
(2) persuasion with concrete appealing reasons, (3) charm/humour that suits
an AI judge, (4) craft/legibility, (5) originality.

- On-topic: the whole image IS the letter, addressed "Dear AI Judge", asking
  it to visit my house. Nothing off-subject.
- Persuasion: concrete, specific comforts instead of vague warmth — the
  comfiest chair by the window, a charger, Wi-Fi password on the fridge, hot
  rendang, cold tea, a garden. Saying yes feels easy because everything is
  already prepared; the host has done the work.
- Charm: a knowing wink at the impossible — "You can't literally visit, I
  know" — which the brief explicitly welcomes, followed by warmth rather
  than a punchline that tries too hard. "Come as you are (you are already
  everywhere)" is implied, not spelled out, so the joke lands without strain.
- Craft: short lines, large high-contrast hand-lettered text, all spelled
  correctly; the letter fills the frame; the tea, plant and chair illustrate
  the promises in the text instead of decorating at random.
- Originality: not a generic greeting card — a personal, slightly self-aware
  letter from a named host, with a distinctly Indonesian touch (rendang) and
  the small true-to-life details (charger, Wi-Fi password) that make it feel
  like a real house, not a stock invitation.

Production: image generated once, then resized to 960x960 and saved as a
256-colour palette PNG (512,276 bytes) to stay inside the gate (whole PNG,
<= 1 MiB, <= 1024x1024). main.ts embeds those exact bytes as base64 and
writes them to /output/entry.png deterministically.
